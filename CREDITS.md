## Career Highlights logos (Data & Engineering page)
Each logo is in its official full colour on a transparent background, scaled to the same visual weight and never distorted. The alt text is the company name.
- **Warner Chappell Music:** the official vector logo from the header SVG on https://warnerchappell.com/. Its gold-foil monogram and white wordmark only work on dark backgrounds. For the light page I used WCM's official single-colour version, which WMG published with the 2019 rebrand (https://www.wmg.com/news/warner-chappell-music-unveils-new-logo-and-brand-identity-34301), in black. File: `astro/public/media/logos/career/warner-chappell-music.svg`.
- **SoundCloud:** the official orange logo, "Logo orange on black", from the SoundCloud Media Kit (https://soundcloud.com/company/media-kit), with the black background removed and the orange set to SoundCloud's #FF5500. File: `astro/public/media/logos/career/soundcloud.png`.
- **Meta:** the official "Positive Primary" company lockup (blue infinity mark plus Meta wordmark) from Meta's Brand Resource Center (https://www.meta.com/brand/resources/meta/company-brand/, the Meta_Company-Lockup.zip download). Only the viewBox was trimmed to the artwork. Meta's guidelines require Brand Review approval for every use of its logo; Jose accepted that risk. File: `astro/public/media/logos/career/meta.webp`.
- **Spark Networks:** the company-supplied logo distributed via PR Newswire (https://mma.prnewswire.com/media/680237/Spark_Networks_Logo.html), in its original navy #162336 and cyan #00A8E1, with the white background removed. spark.net currently shows "Website Expired". File: `astro/public/media/logos/career/spark-networks.png`.
- Spark Networks logo is intentionally unlinked: spark.net returns 404 ("Website Expired") and sparknetworks.com is a parked domain (checked 2026-10-11).
